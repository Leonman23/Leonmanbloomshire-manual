The quest in twelve moves

  1. Fish from minute one. A cast costs 1 energy, a fish pays back more. You are also hunting the Golden Fish for chapter 15 (about 1 cast in 33, town river or home island only). Never fry or sell it.
  2. Fry every common fish. It is your fuel all game.
  3. Plant early. Only harvests give Farming XP.
  4. Clear chapters 1-7. Mostly walking and reading. Chapter 6 needs night (9 PM game time).
  5. Finish crop tasks 1-7 and reach Farming 5 before chapter 8: grow about 5 spare Cabbage.
  6. Stonepeak: loot the shift boss's chest first (Bronze pickaxe), then mine 29 copper: 14 for Dunn, 15 for Bram.
  7. Buy Dunn's 25 Stone in town instead of mining it.
  8. Green Vein: prospector's chest gives a Gold pickaxe and 2 Emeralds. Break 3 seams, grab Haskett's Note.
  9. Raise 15,000 Gold for Bram: sell loot, then fish, then crops. Mine last.
  10. The Gloom: fit the lever handle, mine 2 Rubies, open the red-deep chest.
  11. Sunken Vault: Amethyst rod and pickaxe; mine 1 Gold Ore.
  12. Act III: plant bean and eggplant tasks at once, sow Star Fruit at Farming 10, fish Ghost Catfish after dark.

Energy basics

  • Bar: 100 (150 for NFT holders). It never refills on its own.
  • Bed: full refill after 1 hour, usable every 12 hours (6 for holders), counted from when you lie down. It heals you fully any time.
  • Cooked food restores energy with no daily cap; raw food restores none. Cooking is free.
  • Free: walking, talking, reading, puzzles, chests, hoeing, watering, cooking.
  • Costs: sow 1, harvest 1, axe 1, cast 1, ore blow 2, combat 1 per 3 hits.

Fishing energy

Each landed fish fried returns 5 energy. Above 20% you gain energy.

Mining energy

Doubles give 2 ore for one extra blow, so break them first. For quests that count rocks, break singles.

Gold for Bram

Never sell

  • Golden Fish, Ghost Catfish
  • 5 Wheat, 5 Cabbage, 5 Corn, 2 Rubies, 2 Silvercap
  • Star Fruit seeds and every quest item
  • 3 Emeralds, 15 Copper, 5 Gold Ore until Bram has them

Skip Sell All. Safe once you have spares: Diamond, extra Emeralds and Gold Ore, Amethyst, Quartz, Rice and other crops.

Crop tasks

A task counts only the crop it names, and each crop gives a single harvest. One harvest counts toward every open task that names it. Seeds: Hazel's shop; Marigold and Sunflower at Juniper's.

XP needed

Level 5: 557. Level 6: 851. Level 7: 1,204. Level 8: 1,613. Level 9: 2,079. Level 10: 2,601. The 17 tasks total about 2,600 XP, but Act I leaves you 60-85 XP short of level 5.

Star Fruit

Sow at Farming 10. It takes 5 hours: the timer shows 4, then a second watering unlocks the last hour. Marven also wants 5 Wheat, 5 Cabbage and 5 Corn that you grew.

Shards from crops

  • A rare-crop harvest drops 1-2 Shards about 1 time in 8. Fish: 1 in 20. Ore, trees, foraging: 1 in 25.
  • Rare means seeds that unlock at Farming 10+, plus Saffron and Star Fruit.
  • Best pick: Sweet Potato (level 10, 70 min, seed ~435, sells ~870).
  • Lucky drops cap at 30 Shards a day, resetting 00:00 UTC.

Numbers come from the game's client code as of 3 Oct 2026. The game is in beta, so confirm key figures in play.
